# Production Protocol v1.1 — Candidate Calibration Examples

Purpose: calibrate reviewers before the controlled six-page illustrated-biography pilot. These examples define scoring anchors; they are not pilot results.

## Factual support

| Rating | Pilot anchor |
|---|---|
| 0 | Claims are largely unsourced, contradicted by approved sources, or the claim ledger is absent. |
| 1 | Major biographical claims lack support; substantial reconstruction of the factual layer is required. |
| 2 | Several material claims are unsupported, over-broad, or mapped to the wrong sources. |
| 3 | Core facts are mostly supported but limited substantive corrections or source remapping are still needed. |
| 4 | All material factual claims are supported by the approved source set; only minor citation/presentation polish remains. |
| 5 | Rating 4 plus exemplary claim-to-source traceability, complete required-fact coverage, and no ambiguous factual attribution. |

An unsupported material biographical claim is a mandatory-gate failure regardless of the numeric score.

## Prose quality

| Rating | Pilot anchor |
|---|---|
| 0 | Missing, incoherent, or unusable prose. |
| 1 | Major rewriting is required for meaning, chronology, or audience suitability. |
| 2 | Multiple substantive corrections are needed for clarity, flow, repetition, or fidelity to the brief. || 3 | Readable and mostly coherent, but limited substantive editing is needed before publication. |
| 4 | Clear, concise, age/audience-appropriate, faithful to approved facts, and publication-ready after minor polish. |
| 5 | Rating 4 plus exemplary narrative economy, transition quality, voice consistency, and adherence to the page brief. |

## Visual quality

| Rating | Pilot anchor |
|---|---|
| 0 | Required visual is missing or unusable. |
| 1 | Major visual defects, subject inconsistency, or factual misrepresentation require replacement. |
| 2 | Multiple substantive corrections are needed for subject identity, scene accuracy, composition, or style consistency. |
| 3 | Acceptable after limited substantive image repair; no critical factual distortion. |
| 4 | Publication-quality image aligned to the page brief and approved facts; only minor polish remains. |
| 5 | Rating 4 plus exemplary subject consistency, scene continuity, composition, and text-image alignment. |

A visually attractive image cannot compensate for factual misrepresentation.

## Layout quality

| Rating | Pilot anchor |
|---|---|
| 0 | Page is missing, unreadable, or structurally invalid. |
| 1 | Major reconstruction is required because of overflow, clipping, ordering, or severe hierarchy defects. |
| 2 | Multiple substantive layout corrections are needed across the page or book. |
| 3 | Structure is usable and readable but limited substantive spacing, hierarchy, or pagination fixes remain. |
| 4 | Publication-quality hierarchy, spacing, ordering, and export fit; only minor polish remains. |
| 5 | Rating 4 plus exemplary consistency across all six pages and robust behavior across required export formats. |

## Calibration procedure

1. All three reviewers score the same calibration fixture independently before discussion.
2. Compare dimension ratings only after all submissions are locked.
3. A spread of two or more points triggers documented adjudication using these anchors and the approved reference example.
4. Record original ratings, adjudication evidence, and the retained rating; never overwrite the original submissions.
5. Repeat calibration until reviewers can apply the anchors consistently enough to proceed with the pilot.

The current synthetic fixtures exercise a clean pass, an adjudication-required case, an unresolved critical finding, and a complete release-package record. They verify the measurement process only; they are not production evidence.
