# Production Protocol v1.1 Candidate — Rubric v1.1-r1

Scope: controlled six-page illustrated-biography pilot only.

## Dimensions and weights

- Factual support — 25%
- Prose quality — 25%
- Visual quality — 25%
- Layout quality — 25%

Each dimension is rated 0–5 by exactly three independent reviewers. The release rating is the median. Overall score = sum(weight × median rating ÷ 5).

## Release threshold

A book may pass only when the total score is at least 90/100, every dimension is at least 4/5, all mandatory checks pass, no critical defect remains unresolved, and named editorial approval is recorded.

Unsupported material biographical claims are mandatory-gate failures and cannot be offset by stronger ratings elsewhere.
## Rating anchors

0 — missing or unusable.
1 — major defects; substantial reconstruction required.
2 — multiple substantive corrections required.
3 — acceptable after limited substantive editing.
4 — publication quality; minor polish only.
5 — exemplary against approved reference examples.

Dimension-specific examples are defined by `rating-levels-v1.json` and the calibration examples document. A reviewer spread of two or more points in any dimension requires documented adjudication. Original reviewer submissions must be retained unchanged.

## Critical findings

Any reviewer may raise a critical finding. It blocks approval until resolved or explicitly dismissed with evidence. Critical factual, access-control, preservation, recovery, or spending-limit defects cannot be numerically averaged away.
